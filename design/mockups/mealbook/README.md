# Mealbook mobile UI references

These screenshots were selected from `stitch_nutrition_tracker_pwa_ui_design.zip` as visual references for the existing Mealbook food expense tracker. `logo-inspiration.png` was supplied separately and guides the updated open-book and cutlery symbol in `mealbook-mark.svg`.

- `tracker.png`: monthly meal tracker
- `meal-entry.png`: daily meal entry
- `payments.png`: payments and balances
- `services.png` and `add-service.png`: service list and service form
- `mealbook-logo-mark.png` and `mealbook-mark-original.svg`: original archive brand mark
- `logo-inspiration.png` and `mealbook-mark.svg`: the supplied logo inspiration and the clean vector mark drawn from it

The archive also contains unrelated nutrition/macros and grocery-budget concepts. They are intentionally not included or treated as application requirements. The HTML prototypes are reference-only and are not shipped; they rely on external CDN assets and are not the app implementation.
